Warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia is a rare, life-threatening disease that causes intense fatigue. Johnson & Johnson has a new therapy that targets the underlying drivers of the disease. Here, two patients—and Johnson & Johnson employees—share their journey, from symptoms and diagnosis to managing work and personal life.
